Actionable decisions for the user's machine
Use stock camera and current built-in settings for the clean baseline. Validate each inherited command against the installed build. Preserve enemy/trooper visibility, ability decals, blast-vent effects, Doorman ultimate indicators and readable world text. Do not add an unhider or bypass to force protected commands.

This user's Ryzen 7 9800X3D/RX 7800 XT/32 GB Windows PC differs from most cited reports. DX11 is currently loaded according to the local second audit; compare Vulkan under its own warm-up rather than naming a universal winner. Avoid importing DLSS advice into an AMD profile.

Benchmark dense city views, late-game base fights, shop/scoreboard transitions and first-use abilities, then repeat warm-cache runs. Collect frame times, CPU/GPU busy, RAM/commit and VRAM over session duration. Avoid automatic shader-cache deletion: older instrumented reports show more opening stalls following cache/backend changes. Current local memory pressure is a confound to control, not a proven stutter diagnosis.

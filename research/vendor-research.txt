Vendor research for Deadlock on Ryzen 7 9800X3D + Radeon RX 7800 XT
As of 2026-10-03. Research only; no system mutations.

Practical conclusions
- Use native AMD low latency (r_low_latency 1, confirmed by engine agent) as a latency candidate, and compare its frametimes on/off. NVIDIA Reflex/Boost are inapplicable to this GPU.
- Measure current 1080p output at 100% render scale against game-integrated FSR. Choose lower render scale by actual GPU pressure and readable moving targets/souls, not by an arbitrary promise.
- Keep RSR, VSR, Radeon Boost/Chill, AFMF, Enhanced Sync and driver FRTC out of the first real-FPS comparison. FSR 3 menu text does not itself prove frame generation. Use the game limiter after measuring.
- Leave HAGS, Game Mode, active Ultimate Performance and current driver unchanged for the initial config A/B. These are separate experiments.
- Installed graphics driver 32.0.31041.1004 is Adrenalin 26.8.1. Official 26.9.2 exists (September 29), but its release notes do not establish a Deadlock fix.
- Preserve shader caches during warm runs. Mod-cache remediation is separate from driver/Steam shader cache maintenance.
- Display API confirms physical LG ULTRAGEAR+ primary current output 1920x1080 at 480 Hz and Acer XB273 GZ secondary portrait 1080x1920 at 240 Hz. Virtual SudoMaker outputs are detached. Panel native resolution, FreeSync/VRR and driver caps remain unconfirmed.
- Determine Instant Replay state from settings; an AMD background service is insufficient evidence. Capture on/off is a benchmark control.
- Audit snapshot shows low RAM/commit headroom. Measure page activity alongside frame timing before blaming a config or disabling the page file.

The JSON is the complete decision/source map. All recommended choices are unmeasured candidate settings; no FPS improvement or superiority over OptiLock is claimed.
